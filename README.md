# MELD4AMR

**Molecular Embeddings via Low-Distortions Framework for Antimicrobial Resistance**

MELD4AMR is a German–Serbian research collaboration developing efficient, interpretable, and privacy-aware molecular representations for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) research.

The project brings together expertise in artificial intelligence, mathematical modelling, computational biology, molecular representation learning, and public health research.

## Mission

Antimicrobial resistance is a major public health challenge that requires faster and more responsible approaches to molecular analysis and therapeutic discovery.

MELD4AMR investigates compact molecular embeddings designed to preserve meaningful molecular relationships while reducing computational complexity. The project focuses on three connected research directions:

- **Efficient molecular embeddings** for scalable molecular analysis
- **Interpretability and explainability** for scientifically meaningful molecular representations
- **Privacy-aware collaboration** for responsible analysis of sensitive molecular and surveillance data

Our long-term objective is to support transparent, efficient, and secure molecular AI methods relevant to AMR research, antimicrobial discovery, and public health applications.

## Research Areas

### Low-distortion molecular representations

The project investigates molecular embedding approaches based on low-distortion dimensionality reduction. These methods aim to make high-dimensional molecular data more computationally manageable while preserving relevant structural relationships.

### Interpretable molecular AI

MELD4AMR examines how compact molecular representations can remain understandable to domain experts. This includes work on representation-level interpretability and explainability for molecular analysis.

### Privacy-aware molecular data analysis

The project explores privacy-preserving mechanisms that can support secure molecular data analysis and collaboration across institutions and national borders.
